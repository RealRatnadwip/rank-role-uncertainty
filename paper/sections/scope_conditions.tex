\subsection{Summary of Failure Regimes}
\label{subsec:scope-failures}

Three structural boundary failures require explicit vigilance whenever interpreting \RST{}
outputs:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Near-monogamous skew ($\mathbf{w}_{99}$ failure).} Checking only that $w_k < 1$
        misclassifies strongly skewed portfolios where a primary connection already holds $99\%$
        share and a single outlier sits in contingency. Any empirical classifier must test
        competition rank ties (\texttt{SharedMaximum} up to $\epsilon$-tolerance) or cross-role
        incomparability directly.
  \item \textbf{Time-budget saturation.} Unlimited pool expansion $n \to \infty$ raises
        $\Prob(K \ge 2) \to 1$ monotonically in unconstrained Binomial equations, whereas actual
        maintenance time caps active chat slots ($B_{\max}$). Once saturation sets in, marginal
        optionality shifts partner selection quality rather than multiplying active threads indefinitely.
  \item \textbf{Finite-$n$ percolation misreading.} Applying infinite-tree critical densities
        $q_c$ to $n \le 10$ acquaintance graphs understates sub-threshold multi-partner cascades
        ($29.33\%$ at $q = 0.20 \approx q_{\mathrm{ref}}$ for $n = 8$) while ignoring hub vulnerabilities.
\end{enumerate}
