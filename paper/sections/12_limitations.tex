\section{Threats to Validity and Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Every formal model of human relational coordination introduces abstractions
that constrain empirical interpretation. We document the primary methodological
and mathematical limitations of \RST{} below.

\subsection{Measurement Disturbance and Observer Reactivity}
\label{subsec:lim-measurement}

In classical survey designs, querying a respondent's internal ranking is
assumed to read out a pre-existing preference without altering it. Under \RST{},
that assumption fails fundamentally: asking ``who is your primary partner?''
acts operationally as a poke $\poke$ (\cref{def:poke}). Longitudinal observation
therefore faces an acute reactivity problem: repeated direct elicitation
collapses the very superposed state the researcher seeks to measure. Indirect
behavioral proxies such as weekly messaging cadence and shared-calendar logs
reduce reactivity (\cref{app:instrument}) at the cost of noisy role inference.

\subsection{Selection and Survivorship Bias in Observational Accounts}
\label{subsec:lim-selection}

Naturalistic anecdotes of concurrent relational configurations suffer from two
opposite selection distortions. High-drama collapses (especially social-graph
cascades where partners compare notes) are far more salient and widely reported
than stable, low-conflict superpositions that persist quietly over months.
Conversely, partners who occupy pure contingency ($r_5$) or emotional-support
($r_2$) niches often remain unaware of concurrent connections until collapse
occurs, leading retrospective self-reports to undercount pre-poke multiplicity.

\subsection{Non-Universality and Identifiability of \texorpdfstring{$\kappaS$}{kappa}}
\label{subsec:lim-kappa}

Unlike a physical constant, the situationship constant $\kappaS$ is not
universal across individuals or contexts. In~\eqref{eq:kappa-def},
$\kappaS(\Psi; \opR, \opL)$ depends on the focal individual's communication
framing, the non-commutativity of role expectations in the local social norm,
and the current state amplitudes. Moreover, because $\opR$ and $\opL$ are only
identifiable up to simultaneous unitary basis changes (\cref{prop:unitary-invariance}),
empirical recovery of $\kappaS$ requires fixing a standardized behavioral
measurement protocol for both rank and role dispersions.

\subsection{Role-Basis Granularity and Orthogonality}
\label{subsec:lim-basis}

\cref{tab:roles} adopts a five-role orthonormal basis $\roleset = \{r_1, \dots, r_5\}$
as a tractable approximation. In practice, functional roles overlap: an evening
dinner may combine financial support ($r_3$), entertainment ($r_4$), and primary
visibility ($r_1$). Merging correlated functions reduces effective dimension
$m$, whereas subdividing roles (for instance, separating emotional crisis
processing from daily conversational venting) increases $m$ and modifies
estimated role entropy $H_{\mathrm{role}}(G_i)$. Model calibration therefore
requires validating role orthogonality empirically via factor analysis on
behavioral logs.

\subsection{Independence Assumptions in Retention and Percolation}
\label{subsec:lim-independence}

Both the baseline binomial retention model (\cref{sec:conjecture}) and the
bond-percolation cascade model (\cref{sec:cascade}) assume conditional independence
across edges and options. While v1.2 adds heterogeneous Poisson-binomial and
capacity-capped ($B_{\max} = 3$) retention curves alongside clustered and hub topologies
in \cref{sec:simulations}, triadic closure, endogenous rewiring, and continuous dynamic
time budgets remain outside closed-form analytic solutions.

\subsection{Markovian Memorylessness Versus Adaptation}
\label{subsec:lim-markov}

The attention-drift model of \cref{sec:dynamics} assumes first-order Markov
transitions conditioned only on current attention slot and weekday/weekend/stress
regime. Real partners adapt to neglect: sustained low weights $w_i(t)$ increase
partner $G_i$'s propensity to issue a poke, creating state-dependent feedback
between attention drift and collapse hazard that first-order homogeneous
kernels do not capture.
