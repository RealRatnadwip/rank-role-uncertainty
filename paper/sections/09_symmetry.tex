\section{Symmetry of the Phenomenon}
\label{sec:symmetry}

Nothing in Equations~\eqref{eq:partner-state} to~\eqref{eq:conjecture} depends
on the gender of the observed person. The mirror case, in which a man
maintains several partners in superposition (colloquially the ``playboy''
configuration), is governed by the same state vector under relabeling of the
role basis.

\begin{proposition}[Gender independence]
\label{prop:symmetry}
Relational superposition is a function of optionality, not of gender.
\end{proposition}
