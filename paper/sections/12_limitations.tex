\section{Threats to Validity and Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Measurement problem.} Observing the system disturbs it; direct
        elicitation of status may itself act as a poke.
  \item \textbf{Selection bias.} Informal accounts arise from conversations in
        which collapse or ambiguity is already salient, rather than a
        controlled probability sample.
  \item \textbf{Undetermined constant $\kappaS$.} The situationship constant is
        person-dependent and network-dependent, and its empirical distribution
        remains unmeasured.
  \item \textbf{Basis choice.} Results depend on the granularity and
        orthogonality of the finite role basis $\roleset$.
\end{itemize}
