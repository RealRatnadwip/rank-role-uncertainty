\section{Formal Verification in Lean 4}
\label{sec:verification}

To enforce exact algebraic separation between provable mathematics and heuristic modeling,
the deterministic foundations of \RST{} are formalized in Lean~4.36.0 using pure standard
\texttt{Init} without external dependencies, axioms, or unfinished proof placeholders. In
Version~1.1, the original four flat files were reorganized into $20$ focused submodules under
\texttt{lean/RST/} (plus library root \texttt{lean/RST.lean} and entrypoint
\texttt{lean/Main.lean}), paired with a Python invariant suite in
\texttt{tests/test\_rst\_invariants.py}.

\subsection{Modular Architecture and Zero-Axiom Hygiene}
\label{subsec:ver-arch}

\noindent\layermath{} \layerformal{} Formal proofs are partitioned across six directories:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{\texttt{Foundations/}:} finite summation and counting (\texttt{FiniteSets}),
        Gaussian rationals $\Qnum[i]^d$ and complex Cauchy-Schwarz (\texttt{Vectors}),
        zero-safe exit renormalization and $n=1$ boundary (\texttt{Normalization}), and
        discrete expectation and non-negative variance (\texttt{Probability}).
  \item \textbf{\texttt{Relational/}:} column-normalized role matrices with zero-column
        guards (\texttt{RoleMatrix}), dominance and scheme rank reversal (\texttt{RoleDominance}),
        competition ordinal rank bounds (\texttt{Ranking}), unique maximizers (\texttt{UniqueMaximum}),
        and separation between distributed positive weights and shared maxima via the
        $\mathbf{w}_{99} = (99/100, 1/100)$ witness (\texttt{SharedMaximum}).
  \item \textbf{\texttt{Superposition/}:} real and complex collapse weights (\texttt{Amplitudes}),
        composite tensor state unit norm (\texttt{States}), and idempotent Pythagorean
        coordinate projections with normalized post-projection states (\texttt{Projection}).
  \item \textbf{\texttt{Uncertainty/}:} Hermitian and unitary matrix predicates (\texttt{Operators}),
        diagonal commutation, centered anticommutators, and $U^\dagger D U$ Hermiticity preservation
        (\texttt{Commutator}), plus full finite-dimensional Schr\"odinger and Robertson proofs
        with verified $2\times 2$ and $3\times 3$ witnesses (\texttt{Robertson}).
  \item \textbf{\texttt{Dynamics/}:} stochastic matrix step propagation and convex mixtures
        (\texttt{Markov}), absorbing point-mass invariance and EMA weight preservation
        (\texttt{StateTransitions}), and graph cascade size bounds plus branching $R_0$
        monotonicity (\texttt{NetworkCascades}).
  \item \textbf{\texttt{Optionality/}:} Bernoulli variance bound $p(1-p)\le 1/4$ and
        heterogeneous Poisson-binomial moments (\texttt{Bernoulli}), plus exact Binomial
        $\Prob(K \ge 2)$ increments and conditional monotonicity in attractiveness (\texttt{Binomial}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Selected Lean 4 Proof Excerpts}
\label{subsec:ver-excerpts}

\cref{lst:lean-shared-max} reproduces the exact Lean formalization separating
\texttt{DistributedWeights} (Model~A) from \texttt{SharedMaximum} (Model~B) via $\mathbf{w}_{99}$,
and \cref{lst:lean-schrodinger} shows the division-free finite-dimensional Schr\"odinger
uncertainty proof and its Robertson corollary.

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Lean4,caption={Lean 4 separation between DistributedWeights and SharedMaximum (RST/Relational/SharedMaximum.lean).},label={lst:lean-shared-max}]
def w99 : Weights 2 where
  w | 0 => 99 / 100 | 1 => 1 / 100
  nonneg := by intro i; match i with | 0 => grind | 1 => grind
  sum_one := by simp [sumFin_succ, sumFin_zero]; grind

theorem distributed_not_implies_shared_max :
    ¬ (∀ W : Weights 2, DistributedWeights W → SharedMaximum W) := by
  intro hall
  have h := counterexample_99_1_properties
  exact h.2.2 (hall w99 h.1)
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Lean4,caption={Division-free Schrodinger and Robertson inequalities over rational complex vectors (RST/Uncertainty/Robertson.lean).},label={lst:lean-schrodinger}]
theorem schrodinger_uncertainty {d : Nat} (R L : Mat d)
    (hR : IsHermitian R) (hL : IsHermitian L) (ψ : Fin d → Cpx) :
    ((dotC ψ (comm R L ψ)).im) * ((dotC ψ (comm R L ψ)).im) +
      ((dotC ψ (anticommCentered R L ψ)).re) * ((dotC ψ (anticommCentered R L ψ)).re) ≤
      4 * var R ψ * var L ψ := by
  have hcs := cauchy_schwarz_cpx (centered R ψ) (centered L ψ)
  have hcomm := centered_comm_im R L hR hL ψ
  have hanti := centered_anticomm_re R L hR hL ψ
  rw [← hcomm, ← hanti]
  ...
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Reproduction and Strict Verification Boundary}
\label{subsec:ver-scope}

Running \texttt{make lean} compiles all $44$ build targets under \texttt{lean/}, while
\texttt{make test} executes \texttt{python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v} covering
role normalization, ordinal ties, projection, Markov stochasticity, $\gamma=0$ commutation,
and CSV output invariants.

\noindent\textbf{Explicit epistemic boundary.} Lean~4 verifies purely algebraic, order-theoretic,
and discrete probabilistic propositions over $\Nnum$, $\Qnum$, and $\Qnum[i]^d$. Machine
verification does \emph{not} validate floating-point graph sampling (\texttt{run\_simulations.py}),
prove any empirical dating hypothesis (\cref{conj:optionality}), or establish psychological
realism for Hermitian relational operators.
