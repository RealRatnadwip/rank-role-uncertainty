\section{Proposed Unadministered Survey Instrument}
\label{app:instrument}

Consistent with \cref{sec:limitations}, this instrument has \emph{not} been
administered to human participants and no empirical claims in this monograph
rely on survey responses. It is specified here solely as a concrete protocol
for testing the Attractiveness-Optionality Conjecture (\cref{conj:optionality})
without inducing measurement-driven collapse.

\subsection{Anti-Reactivity Design Principles}
\label{app:inst-principles}

Direct status questions (``rank your current partners from 1 to $n$'') are
excluded from participant prompts because they operationalize a poke $\poke$.
Instead, the instrument captures:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Behavioral interaction logs:} weekly partner counts (pseudonymized
        tokens $P_1, P_2, \dots$), interaction frequency, and time-of-day blocks;
  \item \textbf{Functional tag indicators:} non-exclusive checkboxes mapping
        interactions onto the role basis $\{r_1, \dots, r_5\}$;
  \item \textbf{Network overlap indicators:} whether each pair $(P_i, P_j)$
        shares mutual acquaintances or group chats;
  \item \textbf{Independent optionality proxies:} pre-study self-reported incoming
        dates/invitations over a $30$-day baseline window and optional anonymized
        peer-rated attractiveness scores by an external panel.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Ethical and Privacy Safeguards}
\label{app:inst-ethics}

Any future administration requires institutional review approval, fully informed
consent, local hashing of partner identifiers prior to storage so real names
never enter the dataset, and automated deletion of raw message timestamps after
aggregation into weekly cadence summaries.

\subsection{Pre-Specified Statistical Analysis Plan}
\label{app:inst-analysis}

To test \cref{conj:optionality} without post-hoc data dredging, the primary
confirmatory specification regresses an indicator of sustained superposition
($Y_{it} = \mathbf{1}\{\texttt{W\_ACTIVE\_N}_{it} \ge 2 \text{ across } \ge 3\text{ consecutive weeks}\}$)
on perceived attractiveness $A_i$ and viable pool proxy
$N_i = \texttt{OPT\_INCOMING}_i$ via logistic mixed-effects modeling:
\begin{equation}
  \operatorname{logit}\,\Prob(Y_{it} = 1)
  = \beta_0 + \beta_1 A_i + \beta_2 \log(1 + N_i) + \beta_3 \bar{q}_i + u_i,
  \label{eq:instrument-regression}
\end{equation}
where $\bar{q}_i$ measures baseline social-graph edge density across active
contacts and $u_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_u^2)$ is a participant random
intercept. Under the option-mediation prediction (\cref{subsec:conj-design}),
$\beta_2 > 0$ and $\beta_3 < 0$, while the direct attractiveness coefficient
$\beta_1$ attenuates sharply once $\log(1 + N_i)$ is included.
